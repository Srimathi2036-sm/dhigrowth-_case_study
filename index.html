CLEAN CULTURE

About Us

Clean Culture is a modern daily essentials delivery platform designed to simplify everyday household purchases. Customers can order fresh milk, tender coconuts, protein meals, RO water, oils, and other essentials. The platform supports both one-time orders and flexible product subscriptions. It connects customers, vendors, products, payments, orders, and deliveries in one seamless ecosystem. Our goal was to create a convenient, reliable, and predictable daily delivery experience.

Challenges

Engineering the Clean Culture platform required building a high-concurrency delivery engine capable of handling complex session management, real-time inventory validation, and dynamic checkout calculations.

We architected a dual session system supporting multi-step shopping carts alongside zero-latency reordering, while automatically computing real-time pincode serviceability, GST, and coupon discounts. Furthermore, the platform integrates automated recurring subscription pipelines—connecting customers, vendors, and delivery logistics in one seamless ecosystem.

The Solution

To address the platform's high-concurrency challenges, our team architected a high-performance, fault-tolerant checkout and inventory system.

01

Isolated Checkout Sessions

Implemented a temporary, detached session architecture for Reorders. This allows returning customers to bypass the cart and jump directly to payment while keeping their normal active shopping cart completely untouched.

02

Centralized Calculation Engine

Developed a robust backend checkout service that acts as the single source of truth for pricing, automatically fast-forwarding valid sessions to the Razorpay payment gateway.

03

Strict "All-or-Nothing" Validation

Enforced server-side stock checks before checkout initialization. If a product in a reorder is unavailable, the system gracefully halts the process and notifies the user rather than processing a partial order.
